More Than Just Movies

CineVerse is a movie review and rating website created as a web development project using HTML and CSS.

Our goal is to provide a visually engaging experience where visitors can explore fictional movies, discover genres, read sample reviews and build a temporary watchlist.

We believe cinema connects people through memorable stories, creativity and imagination.
